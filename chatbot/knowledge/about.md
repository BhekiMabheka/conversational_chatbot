# About Mzansi Threads

## Who we are
Mzansi Threads is a proudly South African clothing brand founded in 2019 in Maboneng, Johannesburg, by designer Lerato Khumalo. We make modern clothing inspired by South African heritage – shweshwe, Ndebele geometry, Basotho blankets and township street style. Over 80% of our range is designed and manufactured locally, and we work with more than 40 small makers and artisans across Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal and the Eastern Cape.

## Our values
- Local is lekker: we support South African factories, crafters and suppliers.
- Ubuntu: a portion of every sale (2%) goes to our Threads of Hope sewing skills programme for young people in Soweto and Khayelitsha.
- Quality that lasts: natural fibres like cotton, linen, merino wool and genuine leather wherever possible.
