# Returns, Exchanges and Refunds Policy

## Standard returns
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a refund or exchange. Items must be unworn, unwashed, in their original condition and with all tags attached. Please include your order number (it starts with MZ, e.g. MZ123456).

## Items that cannot be returned
For hygiene reasons we cannot accept returns of underwear, swimwear with the hygiene sticker removed, or pierced earrings. Personalised or custom-made items cannot be returned unless faulty.

## Sale and promotional items
Items bought on sale or with a promo code can be returned within 14 days of delivery. They are refunded at the price you actually paid.

## How to return an item
1. Email hello@mzansithreads.example or WhatsApp 010 123 4567 with your order number and the item(s) you want to return.
2. Choose a return option: free in-store return at any Mzansi Threads store (Johannesburg, Cape Town or Durban), or courier collection from your address for R60 (deducted from your refund).
3. We inspect the item within 2 working days of receiving it.

## Refund timing
Refunds are paid to your original payment method within 5–7 working days after the return is approved. Card refunds can take a further 2–3 days to reflect, depending on your bank. Cash on delivery orders are refunded by EFT to a South African bank account. Original delivery fees are not refunded unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong item.

## Exchanges
Exchanges for a different size or colour are free – we cover the delivery of the replacement. If the new size is out of stock we will offer a refund or store credit.

## Faulty or defective items
In line with the Consumer Protection Act, if an item is defective you may return it within 6 months for a repair, replacement or full refund, including delivery costs. Please send a photo of the fault when you contact us.
