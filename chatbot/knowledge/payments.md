# Payments

## Payment methods
- Credit and debit cards (Visa and Mastercard), processed securely; we never store your full card details.
- Instant EFT from all major South African banks.
- Cash on delivery for orders up to R3 000 (card or cash payable to the courier). Not available for cross-border orders.
- Mzansi Threads gift cards.

## Pricing and VAT
All prices are in South African Rand (ZAR) and include 15% VAT. A VAT invoice is emailed with every order.

## Lay-by
Lay-by is available in-store only: pay a 20% deposit and the balance over up to 3 months. The item is kept for you and collected once fully paid. Lay-by cancellations incur a R50 admin fee.

## Gift cards
Gift cards are available in values from R100 to R5 000, online (e-gift card by email) or in-store. They are valid for 3 years and can be used online and in all our stores.
