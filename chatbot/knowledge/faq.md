# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do I need an account to order?
No, you can check out as a guest. An account lets you track orders and collect Ubuntu Rewards points.

## Can I change or cancel my order?
Orders can be changed or cancelled within 1 hour of being placed. Email hello@mzansithreads.example or WhatsApp 010 123 4567 as soon as possible. Once dispatched, the order can be returned under our returns policy.

## Do you offer gift wrapping?
Yes, gift wrapping in shweshwe-print fabric is available for R35 per order, and you can add a free personal message.

## Do you do corporate or bulk orders?
Yes, for orders of 20+ items (e.g. Heritage Day shirts for a team or matching wedding outfits) we offer discounts of 10–20%. Email corporate@mzansithreads.example.

## Is my item in stock?
Items shown on the website are in stock unless marked otherwise. Our stores carry most of the range, but phone ahead for specific sizes.
