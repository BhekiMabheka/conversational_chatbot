# Size Guide and Garment Care

## Clothing sizes
Our clothing comes in sizes XS to XXL. Measurements (chest / waist in cm):
- XS: 80–84 / 64–68
- S: 86–90 / 70–74
- M: 92–98 / 76–82
- L: 100–106 / 84–90
- XL: 108–114 / 92–98
- XXL: 116–122 / 100–106
Shweshwe dresses and the Heritage Silk Shirt have a relaxed fit – if you are between sizes, size down. The rugby jersey is an athletic fit – if you are between sizes, size up. Accessories are one size.

## Veldskoen sizes
Veldskoen come in UK/SA shoe sizes 3 to 13 (whole sizes only). They fit true to size; the suede stretches slightly with wear.

## Caring for your clothes
- Shweshwe: wash cold (30°C) inside out, the first wash may feel stiff – that is the traditional starch and it softens over time.
- Linen: machine wash at 30°C, line dry in the shade, iron while slightly damp.
- Merino wool and blanket coats: hand wash cold or dry clean, dry flat.
- Suede veldskoen: brush with a suede brush, do not machine wash.
- Beadwork: wipe with a damp cloth, keep away from water.
