# Delivery and Shipping

## Delivery costs
- Standard delivery anywhere in South Africa: R99.
- Free standard delivery on orders of R1 000 or more (after discounts).
- Express next-working-day delivery: R149, available in Johannesburg, Pretoria, Cape Town and Durban metro areas for orders placed before 12:00 on a weekday.
- Click and Collect at any of our stores: free, ready in 1–2 working days.

## Delivery times
- Main centres (Gauteng, Cape Town, Durban, Gqeberha, Bloemfontein): 2–4 working days.
- Regional and outlying areas: 4–7 working days.
Orders placed after 14:00 or on weekends are processed the next working day. Deliveries take longer over Black Friday and the December holidays – allow up to 3 extra working days.

## Tracking your order
You receive an SMS and email with a tracking link as soon as your order is dispatched. You can also WhatsApp 010 123 4567 with your order number.

## International shipping
We currently deliver within South Africa and to Namibia, Botswana, Lesotho and Eswatini only. Cross-border delivery is a flat R350 and takes 7–10 working days. Customs duties are the customer's responsibility. We do not yet ship to other countries.
